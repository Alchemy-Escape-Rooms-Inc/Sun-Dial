  The 5th correct symbol is statistically the one most likely to

GPIO15 ESP32-S3 research (corrects earlier note about strapping):
  - GPIO15 is NOT a strapping pin on the ESP32-S3. Strapping pins
    on the S3 are GPIO 0, 3, 45, 46. GPIO15 was a strapping pin
    (MTDO / ROM debug) on the original ESP32 only.
  - GPIO15 outputs LOW for ~60 us during power-up (chip-level
    glitch). Harmless for our case since we are reading, not
    driving.
  - GPIO15 is ADC2_CH4. ADC2 conflicts with WiFi, but ONLY for
    analog reads. digitalRead / attachInterrupt are unaffected.
  - GPIO15 is not USB or JTAG on the S3.
  - Therefore GPIO15 is electrically fine for INPUT_PULLDOWN +
    RISING-edge interrupts with WiFi running.
  - Remaining hardware suspects for the missing 5th pin (in order):
      1. board-specific routing — check the dev board schematic
         for anything tied to GPIO15 (LED, divider, shared net).
      2. level shifter / divider on the wire to GPIO15.
      3. damaged input from prolonged 5 V abuse — easy test:
         physically swap the GPIO15 wire with a known-good pin
         (e.g. GPIO16 or GPIO17) and update HOUSE_PINS[] to match.
         If the previously-working pin now misses, GPIO15 is fine
         and the issue is upstream wiring. If GPIO15 still misses,
         the input is damaged.
